---
title: "What a store can sell"
description: "Shipped goods, digital downloads, codes and licence keys, event tickets and services, and Subscribe & Save as the recurring form of any of them."
---

A Galactic Core store sells anything that has a price, variants and stock. Every product carries a
**fulfilment kind** that states how the buyer receives it, and a variant can override its product's kind.
The kind decides what checkout asks for, what happens once the order is paid, and how a refund or return
is handled. Everything else about the product (catalogue, pricing rules, promotions, tax, the order, the
accounting entries, the events) works the same way for every kind.

| Kind | `fulfilment_kind` | What the buyer receives | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shipped goods | `shipped` | The item, by delivery or collected at a pickup location | When the merchant ships or hands it over |
| Digital download | `digital` | One or more files, through short-lived download links | Within a minute of payment |
| Code or licence key | `code` | One or more codes from the merchant's code pool | Within a minute of payment |
| Event ticket | `event_ticket` | One QR code per person, or one per group | Within a minute of payment |
| Service | `service` | A confirmation with the merchant's note | Within a minute of payment |

`shipped` is the default. A product created without a kind is shipped goods, and an order line written
before the kind existed reads as `shipped`.

## Reading the kind from the catalogue

Product and variant objects carry `fulfilment_kind`. A variant whose kind is not `shipped` also carries a
`fulfilment` object with the public terms of its kind:

```json
{
  "fulfilment_kind": "event_ticket",
  "fulfilment": {
    "kind": "event_ticket",
    "valid_from": "2027-06-12T18:00:00Z",
    "valid_until": "2027-06-13T01:00:00Z",
    "sale_starts_at": "2027-03-01T10:00:00Z",
    "sale_ends_at": null,
    "admits_per_unit": 1,
    "group_mode": "per_person",
    "max_per_order": 6,
    "entry_label": "VIP entrance",
    "check_in": "single",
    "available": 42
  }
}
```

For an event ticket, `available` is the number of tickets that can still be sold: the lower of the
variant's own stock and the room left in any shared venue capacity it belongs to. `null` means no limit.
The figure is refreshed at least every 30 seconds; checkout checks it again at the moment of purchase. The
catalogue never exposes file locations, code pools or code counts.

## Checkout

`POST /v1/orders` decides delivery per line, on the server.

- **An order with at least one `shipped` line** follows the delivery or pickup flow. The shipping quote and
  any free-delivery threshold count the shipped lines only; the other lines are delivered after payment
  without a shipping charge.
- **An order with no `shipped` line** has `fulfillment_method: "none"`. It needs no shipping address, has no
  shipping quote and no shipping charge, and requires a billing location instead:
  `billing_address.country_code` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2) and `billing_address.postal_code`. A store can
  require the full billing address. The billing location is the tax destination, so sales tax and VAT on
  digital goods follow the buyer. When `fulfillment_method` is omitted it is derived from the lines; a
  request that names `delivery` or `pickup` for an order with nothing to ship is treated as `none`, and one
  that names `none` while a line ships is refused with `400 fulfillment_method_invalid`.
- **Tax.** Each line is taxed with the tax code of its kind (digital goods, services, admission to events),
  which the merchant can change per kind.
- **Order-line fields.** `items[].custom_fields` carries values for the store's order-line fields, such as
  an attendee name or a licence holder. A required field on an event-ticket or code line is enforced
  (`400 line_field_required`). For a group event ticket issued one per person, `items[].attendee_names`
  lists one name per person.
- **Free items.** A digital download or an event ticket can be priced at zero. An order whose total is zero
  and whose lines are all downloads or event tickets is created as paid with `payment_method: "free"`, for
  a signed-in shopper (`x-auth-token`, `x-external-auth` or `x-idp-token`); otherwise it is refused with
  `409 account_required`. Every other kind needs a price above zero.

### Event-ticket rules at checkout

Ticket types (general admission, VIP, student, early bird) are variants of one event product, each with its
own price and stock. Several types can share one venue capacity, so the types together never exceed it.

| Refusal | Status | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `ticket_sale_closed` | 409 | The variant's sale has not started or has ended, for example an early-bird type after its end date |
| `ticket_event_ended` | 409 | The event's validity has passed |
| `ticket_limit_exceeded` | 400 | The order holds more of the variant than its per-order limit |
| `insufficient_stock` | 409 | The variant's stock or the shared venue capacity has no room left |

### Codes at checkout

A code product draws from a pool of codes the merchant has uploaded. Checkout refuses a line the pool
cannot cover with `409 insufficient_stock`, so a paid order always has its codes. Codes are stored
encrypted and are shown only to the buyer and to the merchant, one delivery at a time.

## After payment

Delivery starts once the order is paid, whatever paid it: a card payment, a payment provider's
confirmation, a marketplace order or a free order. Each purchased unit is delivered exactly once, even when
a payment confirmation arrives twice. Delivery never holds up the payment: a unit that cannot be delivered
at once is retried for up to a day and the merchant is told if it still has not been delivered.

The buyer receives one email when their items are ready, with download buttons, their codes and their event
tickets' QR codes. The same items are listed in the buyer's account:

| Method | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/customers/{id}/fulfilments` | The shopper's delivered items across orders, one entry per unit |
| `POST /v1/customers/{id}/fulfilments/{fulfilment_id}/reveal` | The codes of one delivery, in clear; each reveal is recorded and rate-limited |
| `GET /v1/fulfilment/downloads/{fulfilment_id}/{asset_id}` | A `302` to a short-lived link to the file |

The two customer methods take the shopper's credential (`x-auth-token`, `x-external-auth` or `x-idp-token`,
exactly one) and the `fulfilment:read` scope for a Connect key. The download method accepts either the
shopper's credential or the link token `t` from the delivery email, so an email link works without an API
key. A download link is minted on each request and is never stored; the merchant can limit the number of
downloads and the number of days a purchase stays downloadable, and a refused download answers `410` with
`link_expired`, `limit_reached`, `voided` or `asset_unavailable`.

### Event tickets at the door

Each ticket's QR code carries a random token; only its hash is stored. Staff admit holders from GC Admin or
the app by scanning the QR code with the device camera or a handheld scanner, or by searching for the
holder's name or order number. A ticket admits once, or once per day for a multi-day pass, and a check-in
shows the holder's name, the ticket type's entry label and the number of people admitted. A group ticket is
issued either as one QR code admitting the whole group or as one QR code per person.

## Refunds and returns

A refund or a processed return cancels the deliveries of the lines it covers: download links stop working,
issued codes are cancelled (never put back on sale), and event tickets stop admitting. An event ticket that
has already been used to check in is not cancelled. The merchant chooses which kinds accept returns (by
default shipped goods and event tickets) and how long before an event its tickets can still be returned.

## Subscribe & Save

[Subscribe & Save](/subscribe-save) is the recurring form of any of these kinds. A renewal is an order like
any other: a renewal of downloads, codes, event tickets or services has `fulfillment_method: "none"`, takes
its billing location from the first order and is delivered once per cycle.

## Events

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `order_item.fulfilled` | A purchased unit was delivered |
| `order_item.fulfilment_failed` | A unit could not be delivered within the retry window |
| `order_item.fulfilment_voided` | A unit was cancelled by a refund, a return or the merchant |
| `event_ticket.checked_in` | An event ticket was admitted |
| `code_pool.low` | A code pool reached its low-stock threshold |

Payloads and delivery are described in [Webhooks](/webhooks).
